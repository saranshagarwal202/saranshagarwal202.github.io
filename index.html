About

My research journey is fueled by a strong desire to explore state-of-the-art technologies and develop innovative solutions to real-world challenges.

I am particularly drawn to the transformative potential of these areas to revolutionize how we interact with and understand the world. My research endeavors are focused on developing robust and versatile AI systems that can seamlessly integrate diverse data sources and perform complex reasoning tasks.

Research Focus

Natural Language Processing and LLMs: My research at the FLAIR Lab centers on enhancing the robustness and reliability of LLMs. I am actively involved in cutting-edge research exploring unlearning techniques, which aim to selectively remove or modify specific knowledge within LLMs. This critical area of research has profound implications for addressing concerns related to privacy, bias, and model adaptability.

Multimodal Models: I am deeply interested in developing multimodal AI systems that can seamlessly integrate and leverage information from diverse sources, such as text, images, and sensors. My research focuses on exploring how models can effectively fuse and align information across different modalities, enabling them to achieve a deeper understanding and perform more sophisticated reasoning tasks.

ML for Remote Sensing and Geospatial Analysis: I am also interested in exploring the application of machine learning techniques to geospatial data. My project on predicting cotton yields using satellite imagery demonstrates my ability to leverage machine learning for analyzing and interpreting geospatial information to address real-world challenges.
